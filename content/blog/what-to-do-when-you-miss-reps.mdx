What to do when you miss reps: first, log the reps you actually completed. Next session, repeat the same weight and try again. If you miss the same target two or three sessions in a row, lower the weight slightly or switch to a rep range. One missed set is normal and does not mean you are getting weaker.

## Why missed reps happen

Missed reps are part of progressive overload. Every time you add weight, there is a chance you will fall short. Common training reasons:

- The weight jump was too big.
- Rest between sets was shorter than usual.
- Earlier sets or exercises were harder than normal.
- An off day.

## What to do when you miss reps during a session

1. **Finish the set safely.** Rack the weight; do not grind out a rep with broken form.
2. **Log the real numbers.** "60 kg x 8, 8, 6" is more useful than pretending you hit 8.
3. **Keep the rest of the session as planned.** You can lower the weight for remaining sets if form slips.

## What to do next session

| Situation | Next step |
| --- | --- |
| Missed reps once | Repeat the same weight |
| Missed the same target twice | Repeat once more or use a smaller jump |
| Missed it three times | Lower the weight about 5 to 10 percent and build back |
| Missing on many lifts | Check for fatigue; see [fatigue vs plateau](/blog/fatigue-vs-plateau) |

## Adjusting your plan

If you keep missing after weight jumps, the jumps may be too big. Try smaller increments, or move from a fixed rep target to a range and use [double progression](/blog/double-progression). [Linear vs double progression](/blog/linear-vs-double-progression) explains the switch.

## An example

Illustrative bench numbers, target 3 sets of 5:

| Session | Weight | Reps | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 70 kg | 5, 5, 3 | Repeat |
| 2 | 70 kg | 5, 5, 4 | Repeat |
| 3 | 70 kg | 5, 5, 5 | Add weight |

Two repeat sessions were enough to complete the target. Logging the real numbers showed that progress was happening even before all sets hit 5.

## Safety

Use a spotter or safety arms on heavy sets, especially when you are near your limit. If a lift causes pain, stop and consult a qualified professional.
