When your bench press stalls, keep the lift in the program, change one thing, and judge that change after two to four weeks. A stall means the same weight and reps for several sessions in a row. One missed set after a rushed week is a normal bad day.

The [bench press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bench_press) is a small-muscle lift compared with a squat or deadlift, so the jumps that still work on a lower-body exercise can be too large here. That is a common reason the bar stops moving while other lifts continue.

## What to do when your bench press stalls

1. **Confirm it in the log.** Three or more sessions at the same weight and reps, with the sessions actually completed, is enough to call it a stall. The wider guide to [strength plateaus](/blog/strength-plateaus) uses the same test for any lift.
2. **Shrink the next jump.** If you have been adding 5 kg, try 2.5 kg, or the smallest plate change your gym allows. A jump you cannot finish becomes a stall that lasts for weeks.
3. **Let reps move first.** Switch a fixed 3x5 to a range such as 5 to 8 and use [double progression](/blog/double-progression). Add weight only after every set hits the top of the range.
4. **Adjust one volume knob.** Add a single set if the current sets all finish cleanly but never progress. If every set is a grind and other lifts are sliding too, a short deload is the more useful change.
5. **Keep the setup the same.** Grip width, pause, and bench angle should stay put while you test a new load. Changing the exercise and the weight in the same week leaves you with nothing to compare.

[What to do when you miss reps](/blog/what-to-do-when-you-miss-reps) covers a single failed set. A stall is that pattern repeating.

## An example

Illustrative bench work sets:

| Week | Plan | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 to 3 | 3x5 at 80 kg | 5, 4, 4 each week |
| 4 | 3x6 to 8 at 75 kg | 8, 7, 6 |
| 6 | 3x6 to 8 at 77.5 kg | 8, 7, 7 |
| 8 | 3x6 to 8 at 80 kg | 7, 7, 6 |

By week 8, 80 kg produced more completed reps than during the stall. The program did not need a new press variation.

If benching causes pain, stop and consult a qualified professional.
