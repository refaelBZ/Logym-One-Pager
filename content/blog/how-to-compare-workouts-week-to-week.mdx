To compare workouts week to week, match the same exercise, the same order of sets, and the same rep target, then see which number moved. A fair comparison is specific. A heavy day set next to a light day, or a new exercise set next to an old one, can make a normal week look like a stall.

## How to compare workouts week to week

Open last week's entry before you start, then check it again after the last set.

1. **Match the exercise name.** "Bench press" and "pause bench" are different lifts. Compare each one with itself.
2. **Match the set type.** Warm-up sets change with how you feel. Working sets are the line that should beat last week. [How to log sets during your workout](/blog/how-to-log-sets-during-your-workout) is worth reading if warm-ups and work sets are mixed together.
3. **Read reps at a given weight, or weight at a given rep target.** If both changed, note which one you meant to progress. More reps at the same weight is a win. Fewer reps at a heavier weight can also be a win if that was the plan.
4. **Skip a broken match.** A week you were away, a deload, or a day you cut short is context, not a new baseline. Compare the next full session with the last full one.

[How to track your workouts](/blog/how-to-track-your-workouts) is the habit that makes the two weeks exist on the page. [How to review your training week](/blog/how-to-review-your-training-week) is the five-minute pass once the numbers are comparable.

## A fair week and an unfair one

Illustrative squat working sets:

| Session | Sets | Fair read |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday, week 1 | 100 kg x 5, 5, 5 | Baseline |
| Monday, week 2 | 100 kg x 5, 5, 6 | Better: one extra rep |
| Friday, week 2, lighter day | 80 kg x 8, 8, 8 | Different session, not a drop from 100 kg |

The Friday workout is useful. It is not evidence that Monday got worse. People make that mistake when they sort the log by date and read the latest number only.

## What "better" can look like

Better this week can be the third set moving from 7 reps to 8, the same 5 reps at 2.5 kg more, or every planned set completed when last week you cut the last one. Pick one of those before the session so you are not hunting for a new story afterward.

If a lift causes pain, stop and consult a qualified professional.
