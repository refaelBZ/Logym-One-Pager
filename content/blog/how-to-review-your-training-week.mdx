To review your workout log each week, spend five minutes looking at your main lifts: compare this week's sets with last week's, note whether reps or weight went up, and set one clear target per lift for next week. A short review turns a list of numbers into a plan. The free [weekly training review](/tools/weekly-training-review) walks through that same check.

## Why a weekly review helps

Logging tells you what happened. Reviewing tells you what to do next. Once a week is often enough to spot a trend without overreacting to one off day. It also catches problems early, such as a lift that has been flat for three sessions.

## How to review your workout log in five steps

1. **Pick your main lifts.** Three to five exercises is enough, usually the first lift of each workout.
2. **Compare with last week.** For each lift, look at weight and reps on every set.
3. **Mark the direction.** Up, same, or down.
4. **Check the context.** Missed sessions, a new exercise, shorter rest, or a different machine can explain a change.
5. **Set one target.** One more rep, a small weight increase, or repeating the same numbers with better form.

That is it. The goal is a quick decision, not a full analysis.

## What to look for

Signs things are going well:

- Reps or weight rising on most main lifts.
- The same numbers feeling easier.
- Sessions completed as planned.

Signs to adjust:

- A lift flat or dropping for two to three weeks.
- Several missed sessions.
- Form changes to hit numbers.

If a lift keeps stalling, read [strength plateaus](/blog/strength-plateaus) and [why am I not getting stronger](/blog/why-am-i-not-getting-stronger).

## An example

An illustrative weekly review:

| Lift | Last week | This week | Next week's target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Squat | 80 kg x 6, 6, 5 | 80 kg x 6, 6, 6 | 80 kg x 7 on set 1 |
| Bench press | 60 kg x 8, 8, 7 | 60 kg x 8, 8, 8 | 62.5 kg x 8 |
| Row | 50 kg x 10, 10, 9 | 50 kg x 10, 9, 9 | Repeat, keep form |

Two lifts moved and one dipped slightly. The plan is simple: push the two that are moving and repeat the row.

## Make it a habit

Pick a fixed time, such as after your last session of the week. Keep the review short so you actually do it. If your log is in good shape, five minutes is plenty.
