A good full body workout plan for beginners uses a few basic exercises, trains the whole body in each session, and repeats two or three times a week. The plan below alternates two workouts, A and B, over three days a week. Each session takes about 45 to 60 minutes. The same kind of plan is in the free [workout templates](/tools/workout-templates).

## The plan

Do 3 sets of 8 to 12 reps for each exercise, unless noted. Rest long enough between sets to do the next one with good form.

**Workout A**

| Exercise | Sets x reps |
| --- | --- |
| Goblet squat | 3 x 8-12 |
| Dumbbell bench press or [push-up](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Push-up) | 3 x 8-12 |
| Seated cable row | 3 x 8-12 |
| Dumbbell lunge | 2 x 8-10 per leg |
| Plank | 2 x 20-30 seconds |

**Workout B**

| Exercise | Sets x reps |
| --- | --- |
| Romanian deadlift (dumbbells or barbell) | 3 x 8-12 |
| Dumbbell [overhead press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overhead_press) | 3 x 8-12 |
| Lat pulldown | 3 x 8-12 |
| Leg press | 2 x 10-12 |
| Dumbbell biceps curl | 2 x 10-12 |

## The weekly schedule

Alternate the workouts with a rest day between each session:

| Week | Monday | Wednesday | Friday |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A | B | A |
| 2 | B | A | B |

If three days do not fit, do A and B twice a week instead. For more on scheduling, see [how often beginners should work out](/blog/how-often-should-beginners-work-out).

## How to pick your weights

For each exercise, choose a weight you can lift for at least 8 clean reps on every set, with a couple of reps left. Start light in the first week while you learn the movements. Swap an exercise for a similar one if your gym lacks the equipment, then keep the swap so your numbers stay comparable.

## How to progress

Use [double progression](/blog/double-progression). Stay at the same weight and add reps until you reach 12 on all sets, then add the smallest weight jump available and work back up from 8. The guide to [progressive overload for beginners](/blog/progressive-overload-for-beginners) covers how to keep the steps small.

## Track every workout

Write down the weight and reps of every set, then check last session's numbers before you start. After a few weeks, you will see exactly which lifts are moving. The guide on [how to log sets during your workout](/blog/how-to-log-sets-during-your-workout) keeps it quick. If an exercise causes pain, stop and consult a qualified professional.
