How to choose weight at the gym: pick a load you can lift for your target number of reps with good form, finishing each set with about 2 to 3 reps left in the tank. If you could do many more reps, go heavier next set. If your form breaks before the target, go lighter. Then log the weight and build from there.

## Start with the rep target

Your plan sets the reps, for example 3 sets of 8 to 12. The right weight is the one that lets you land inside that range with good form. The same weight will not work for every exercise, so find it separately for each one.

## How to choose weight at the gym in four steps

1. **Start light.** Use an empty bar, light dumbbells, or a low machine setting for a warm-up set.
2. **Add weight in small steps** across a few sets until the target reps feel challenging.
3. **Check reps left.** At the end of a set, ask how many more good reps you could do. About 2 to 3 is the target. [RPE vs RIR](/blog/rpe-vs-rir) explains this rating.
4. **Write it down.** That weight is your starting point for next session.

## Too light, too heavy, or right

| Sign | What it means | Next set |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Could do 5 or more extra reps | Too light | Add weight |
| About 2 to 3 reps left, clean form | Right | Keep it |
| Form breaks before the target reps | Too heavy | Lower the weight |

## An example

Illustrative first session on a dumbbell bench press, target 8 to 12 reps:

| Set | Weight per hand | Reps | Reps left |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 8 kg | 12 | About 6 |
| 2 | 12 kg | 12 | About 3 |
| 3 | 14 kg | 10 | About 2 |

Set 3 found the working weight: 14 kg for 10 reps with 2 left. Next session starts there.

## After you find your weight

Use the same weight next time and try for more reps. When you reach the top of the range on every set, move up. [When to add weight vs reps](/blog/when-to-add-weight-vs-reps) and [progressive overload for beginners](/blog/progressive-overload-for-beginners) explain the next steps. If you are new, [your first 4 weeks in the gym](/blog/first-4-weeks-in-the-gym) puts this into a full plan.

## Common mistakes

- Copying the weight someone else uses.
- Going heavy on day one and losing form.
- Not writing it down, then guessing next time.

If a lift causes pain, stop and consult a qualified professional.
